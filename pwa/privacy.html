Privacy

Left or Right is a small game. It keeps only what it needs to run leaderboards fairly, and nothing is sold or used for ads. No cookies are used.

What is stored

WhatWhy
Your nickname (or a guest name like “Guest 4821”)To show you on leaderboards
Your game results: scores, answers and how long each tookTo check every score is real and to show your stats
A random player code, kept on your device. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copyTo recognise you without an email or password
A scrambled (salted hash) form of your network address, never the address itselfTo stop mass sign-ups and spam
Error reports: the error message, the page and your browser typeTo fix problems
If you turn on reminders: your browser’s notification address and your time zoneTo send the Daily reminder at 6 pm your time

Visitor counts

Page visits are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn’t identify you.

Where it’s kept

Game data is stored with Supabase (Sydney, Australia). The site is hosted by Vercel. Fonts and icons are served from this site, not from third parties.

How long

  • Your player, nickname and finished games: until you delete them.
  • Games that were started but never finished: removed after 2 days.
  • Games that failed the score check, and error reports: removed after 30 days.

Deleting your data

Open Your stats (the chart button), then Delete my player. This removes your nickname, every game you played and your reminders, straight away and for good. Clearing the site’s data in your browser removes what’s stored on your device.
